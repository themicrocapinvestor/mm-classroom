# Module 6: RRG Analysis with StockCharts

Some stocks rise while the whole market rises, and some rise faster than everything around them. This module teaches you a colourful map called a Relative Rotation Graph, or RRG, that shows which parts of the market are gaining strength and which are losing it. You will learn to read it on [StockCharts.com](https://www.stockcharts.com), and you will learn where it can fool you.

1. [Relative strength: beating the benchmark, not just rising](/m06/c01/) - why going up is not enough; a stock has to beat the market to be truly strong.
2. [What an RRG is: the four quadrants in plain words](/m06/c02/) - the map with four zones, and what each zone says about a stock or sector.
3. [Reading rotations: leading, weakening, lagging, improving](/m06/c03/) - how stocks travel clockwise through the zones, and how to follow their trails.
4. [RRG with single stocks](/m06/c04/) - plotting Apple and its giant neighbours against the S&P 500, and why single stocks are jumpier than sectors.
5. [RRG plus fundamentals: rotation tells you when, filings tell you what](/m06/c05/) - combining the map with SEC filings so you never act on a pretty picture alone.
6. [When RRG misleads: limits and false rotations](/m06/c06/) - the honest chapter: where this tool fails, and how to protect yourself.
