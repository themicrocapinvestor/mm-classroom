# Learn to read companies the way the government makes them confess.

The Microcap Minute Classroom is a free course in understanding US-listed
companies, built on one idea: **the filing beats the news**. Every lesson comes
from public SEC documents, explained in plain language, with worked examples on
real companies and screenshots of the exact pages you will use.

No prior knowledge assumed. No account, no paywall, no drip. Pick a module and start.
