# Module 4: Reading Event Filings

The big yearly reports tell you how a company looks once a year. But companies keep living between those reports: bosses change, results land, insiders trade, new shares are born. This module teaches you the forms that carry those events, and ends with you hunting real filings yourself.

1. [The 8-K: the something-just-happened form](/mm-classroom/m04/c01/): the short note every company must pin within four business days of big news.
2. [The items map: 1.01, 2.02, 5.02, 8.01](/mm-classroom/m04/c02/): read the label on the note before the note itself.
3. [The proxy statement DEF 14A: pay, owners and power](/mm-classroom/m04/c03/): the once-a-year booklet showing what bosses earn and who really owns the company.
4. [Form 4: when insiders trade their own stock](/mm-classroom/m04/c04/): the two-day confession insiders must file, and the code letters that explain it.
5. [New shares being born: S-1s, 424B5s and offerings](/mm-classroom/m04/c05/): how shares are created, why your slice shrinks, and when that is fine.
6. [Try it: hunting events with our Filings Search page](/mm-classroom/m04/c06/): a hands-on scavenger hunt across everything this module taught you.
