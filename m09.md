# Module 9: Screener Craft

The US market lists thousands of companies, and nobody can read them all. A screener is the sieve that turns ten thousand names into a shortlist you can actually study. In this module you will learn to build that sieve well, dodge its traps, and verify every survivor in the real filings before it earns a minute of your time.

1. [What a screener does: a filter for ten thousand stocks](/mm-classroom/m09/c01/) - how a screener shrinks a giant market into a small pile of candidates, and why that pile is only a set of questions.
2. [Value filters, quality filters, growth filters, in plain words](/mm-classroom/m09/c02/) - the three big filter families, explained with a kirana store.
3. [Building a watchlist and a checklist you actually follow](/mm-classroom/m09/c03/) - a short list with written reasons, plus the five questions you ask every single time.
4. [Screen traps: cheap is not the same as good](/mm-classroom/m09/c04/) - value traps, hidden debt, one-time profits, and cyclicals at the top.
5. [From screen to filing: every candidate gets verified on EDGAR before it earns your time](/mm-classroom/m09/c05/) - the fifteen-minute routine that checks every candidate against the company's own reports.
6. [Try it: a quality-at-a-fair-price screen over the S&P 500](/mm-classroom/m09/c06/) - build the full screen yourself and run one survivor through the whole loop.
